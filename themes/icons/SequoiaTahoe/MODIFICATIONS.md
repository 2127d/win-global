# Sequoia Tahoe

Derived from Vince Liuice’s MacTahoe-blue icon theme.
See AUTHORS for attribution and COPYING for the GPL-3.0 license.

Modified 2026-09-21 for Sequoia Eleven: small monochrome action, status,
device, category, place and emblem contours receive proportional extra
weight and round caps/joins. Full-color application/folder art is retained.
Theme names and shared-family links are updated for independent installation.
Reproducible transformation: tools/build-tahoe-icons.py in the parent project.

## Rounded controls

2026-10-24: UI controls and tray indicators use Google Material
Symbols Rounded, weight 400, with 20/24 optical sizes. Selected states use filled variants.
Adaptations add semantic icon names, optical insets and KDE palette recoloring.
Original Sequoia battery, Wi-Fi and microphone families preserve device states.
Peripheral batteries and cellular/connection/trust indicators preserve their states.
Bare app identities retain Tahoe art; colliding controls use symbolic names.
See material-icons.json, MATERIAL-SYMBOLS.txt and COPYING.MaterialSymbols.
